# Variant route validation: available evidence and gaps (20 November 2026)

Scope: route 8 candidate directions, route 35 extension to vereda Cay, route 48 extension to Juntas, and the revised route 28 direct Caiké access. This record does **not** authorize GTFS geometry or timetable changes.

## Evidence layers and rules

- **Priority A, official geometry:** TransMusical 2026 route posters, as **raster route maps with labelled reference landmarks**, not downloadable coordinate-by-coordinate GTFS shapes or KML.
- **Priority A, official operation:** Ibagué Secretaría de Movilidad, [Resolución 005549 of 9 October 2026](https://ibague.gov.co/portal/admin/archivos/normatividad/2026/69845-RES-20261009145629.pdf), pp. 7–8 for the conditional 48 Juntas dispatch rule, and [municipal itinerary change notice of 18 October](https://www.ibague.gov.co/portal/noticias/23334-este-lunes-cambiaran-los-recorridos-de-14-rutas-de-busetas-en-ibague-conozca-sus).
- **Priority B, old stop sequences:** SHA-256-pinned May 2026 Universidad de Ibagué source: route 8 patterns R9/R10/R11/R12, 35 R45/R46, 48 R53/R54, 28 R37/R38. The source data is not automatically proof of current October 2026 route branches.
- **Priority C, OSM road feasibility:** [14-route GraphHopper candidates](graphhopper_full_2026_11_20.md) and [four Cay/Juntas branch candidates](route8_cay_juntas_routing_review.md). Successful routing only proves the candidate road can be traversed under the server's OSM `bus` profile.
- **Priority D, secondary branch stop order, not legal authority:** [Moovit route 48 toward Tierra Firme](https://moovitapp.com/index/es-419/transporte_p%C3%BAblico-line-48-Ibagu%C3%A9-4506-957721-86260811-1), and [Transitrun route 48 toward Juntas](https://transitrun.com/es/public-transit-line_48_58613). These list the end-to-end Juntas stop corridor but may reflect **older city streets** prior to October 2026. Never treat their clock times/headways as overriding Resolución 005549.

## Geometry validation policy

For each **actual** operating pattern/direction:
1. Record whether the **official route map** explicitly shows the variant or only mentions it in a note. If it only mentions it, mark official branch geometry **unverified**.
2. Annotate official landmark sequence in both directions and the changed October 2026 streets.
3. Resolve physical boarding stops and order, including right-hand side and repeated/circular street passages.
4. Constrain GraphHopper with **officially evidenced via points**; the existing stop-only GraphHopper shapes have **zero officially reviewed October waypoint anchors** and must not be called validated routes.
5. Audit road/stop distance, directional correctness, visit order, continuity, extra detours, shared corridor/branch split, and candidate-to-official-map deviation.
6. Only then assign distinct GTFS `shape_id`, stop_times and frequency shares. The headway is the **combined corridor service**, not a fresh complete headway for each branch.
7. Do not infer 2026 operational timetables from the secondary stop lists. Keep all provisional variants in the research register until the real branch service has been confirmed.

**Bottom line:** Yes, route 8 has sufficient official mapping to validate direction and the Calle 100 detour, but not the operational status of its two short patterns; route 48 has a useful candidate **Juntas stop order** plus official dispatch policy, so its rural geometry can be checked after establishing real stopping points; route 35's Cay branch lacks a genuine, source-backed ordered stop sequence, despite the official one-in-three rule. Route 28's new park access is a standard-route geometry review, not yet proof of another timetable variant.
